My name is **Akhilendra Verma**. I am an Irish-Indian living in Ireland, working as a software project manager at an investment bank. I love photography, and I write stories drawn from memory — of people, places, and small moments that stayed with me.

This blog, *Expression By Akhil*, is a collection of those pieces: personal essays and memoirs, mostly in Hindi, about growing up in India and the life I carry with me now.

Thank you for reading.

— Navan, Meath, Ireland
